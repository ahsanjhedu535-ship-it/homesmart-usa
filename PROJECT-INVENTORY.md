## Implementation notes
- All illustrations are local SVG files; no third-party image hotlinks are required.
- Calculators run client-side in `assets/main.js`.
- Canonical URLs use `homesmartusa.example` placeholders and must be replaced before launch.
- Article JSON-LD uses the organization as author; no individual author or unsupported date is fabricated.
- Ad areas are labeled placeholders, not active ads.
