About HomeSmart USA

Learn about HomeSmart USA's mission to make home energy, maintenance, and improvement information clearer for everyday homeowners.

Advertisement placement · optional approved code

Our purpose is to make home energy, maintenance, and improvement information easier to understand. HomeSmart USA is designed as an independent educational resource for homeowners and people planning household projects.

Our approach

We aim to explain the choices, assumptions, and trade-offs behind common home decisions. Our free calculators use inputs entered by visitors and perform simple calculations in the browser. They do not connect to utility accounts, collect calculator entries on a server, or replace professional inspections and quotes.

What you can expect

We prioritize clarity, practical steps, transparent uncertainty, and references to official resources when rules or technical details may change. We do not claim professional credentials, guarantee savings, or promise search rankings or advertising revenue.
