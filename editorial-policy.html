Our standards

We prefer official and primary sources for technical guidance, safety recommendations, energy programs, and rules that may change. Relevant sources may include the U.S. Department of Energy, ENERGY STAR, the EPA, national laboratories, utilities, state agencies, and local building departments. Readers should verify time-sensitive rules at the official source.

Transparency and responsibility

We do not use fabricated reviews, testimonials, authors, credentials, or statistics. Calculator outputs are based on disclosed user inputs and simple formulas. Estimates are not guarantees. Where a page has not been independently reviewed on a stated date, we do not invent a review date.
